From Jinja to the World

I am a Ugandan academic, diplomat and writer based between Jinja City and abroad. I teach Information Systems and Technology, connecting Africa's innovation potential with global digital transformation.


Alumnus of Makerere University (Africa's premier institution) and the University of Oxford, my work lies at the intersection of technology, social capital, and governance.

My Vision for the United Nations

The UN must evolve from a post-war institution to a post-digital, people-centered institution. I propose:

  • 1. Digital Equity: Internet as a human right
  • 2. Social Capital First: Governance through community
  • 3. South-North Balance: True representation
  • 4. Governance for Life: Sustainable, not seasonal leadership
